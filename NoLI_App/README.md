# NoLI™ App: The Logic Map Workspace
### *Non-Lie Logic Intent (NoLI) — Powered by IfThenWhy™*

---

## 1. Purpose & Functional Intent
The **NoLI™ App** is a tool-agnostic workspace designed to assist users in **making, updating, testing, and auditing** their own **Logic RFC™** map files. It provides a structured environment for humans to define their business logic (SEM, BRG, LDD, DIC, and LUT). 

By standardizing these rules, NoLI ensures that business logic remains completely transparent to human stakeholders asking "What is the logic behind this metric?", while staying structured enough for machine and AI agents to reference without drift.

## 2. Core Capabilities
This app is designed for "Worker Bee" metadata capture, allowing users to build, clarify, and manage their own institutional wisdom:

* **Forge (Define/Update):** A manual interface to capture and refine business rules within the **Logic RFC™** text-based architecture.
* **Persona Translation (View):** A toggle to help users visualize their defined math (LDD) alongside their intended business purpose (SEM). This allows business people to immediately audit the "Why" behind any metric or task without reading code.
* **Hallucination Audit (Testing Utility):** Provides **BERT** and **RAGAS** scoring to help users test if their own map files are structurally clear, non-ambiguous, and resistant to semantic drift.
* **Integrity Checks:** Tools to help users manually validate the links between their **Lookup Tables (LUT)** and **Data Dictionaries (DIC)**.

## 3. Thought Leadership Protocol
The **Logic RFC™** framework and the **NoLI™ App** represent a **Thought Leadership Protocol**, not a proprietary service or a guaranteed fail-safe. This methodology prioritizes human intent over technical infrastructure, providing a simple, permanent standard for documenting "Why" data has value.
