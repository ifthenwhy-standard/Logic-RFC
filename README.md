### 🎯 The "IfThenWhy" Intent
The **IfThenWhy™** framework prioritizes **Intent over Infrastructure**. It ensures that every AI-generated action is mathematically tethered to a human-signed business requirement, preventing **Logic Drift** and unauthorized autonomous actions.

### 📂 Repository Structure (The 6-File Logic DNA™)

1. **MAN (Metric Manifest):** - **Function:** The Identity Anchor and Version Control.
   - **NIST Alignment:** Implements **Automated Activity Logging** to track system ownership and identity across distributed infrastructure.

2. **SEM (Semantic Layer):** - **Function:** The "Business Why" and If-Then triggers.
   - **NIST Alignment:** Provides **Human Factors Monitoring** by ensuring AI logic remains transparent and interpretable to human stakeholders.

3. **LDD (Logical Data Design):** - **Function:** The "Hardened Math" and Validation Logic.
   - **NIST Alignment:** Enforces **Input Validation & Predictable Behavior** (NIST SP 800-53 SI-10) to ensure mathematical outputs never deviate from the "Gold Standard."

4. **ERD (Logical ERD):** - **Function:** The blueprint of entity relationships and logic flow.

5. **DIC (Data Dictionary):** - **Function:** The physical **Supply Chain Provenance** map.

6. **LED (Provenance Ledger):** - **Function:** The immutable "History Book" of logic execution.
   - **NIST Alignment:** Enables **Decision Reconstruction**, allowing auditors to verify every hash and UAT pass in seconds for forensic accountability.

* **WHY:** To provide **Non-Repudiation** and ensure the AI remains grounded in the **IfThenWhy™ Standard of Truth**.
